Our Story

Built to Change What Home Construction Looks Like.

In 2017, Sripad Nandiraj returned to India and co-founded MAKAAN WALEY with Rohan Vinayak Reddy — to make the home-building experience worthy of the home itself. One contract. One fixed price. One project manager per site. A live dashboard so every client sees every milestone, material delivery, and quality checkpoint — without visiting the site.

What started in Hyderabad now spans 8 cities, with a ₹250 Cr+ order book, ₹50 Cr+ raised, and 500+ families who trusted us with the biggest financial decision of their lives. That trust shapes every decision we make.

“We believed there was a better way to build homes. Eight years and 400+ projects later, we’re still proving it.”

— Sripad Nandiraj, Co-Founder & CEO
Our Values

What We Stand For. Every Project, Every Day.

Five principles that shape every quote, every site, and every home we hand over.

01

Transparency

Pricing, process, and progress — visible to every client, always. Traceable quotes, documented materials, real-time milestones. No fine print. No surprises.

02

Accountability

When something needs fixing, we fix it. The 10-year structural warranty has a claims process. The rent-back clause is in your contract — not just the conversation.

03

Precision

24 milestones. 250+ quality checkpoints. Licensed inspections at every critical stage. Homes built to last 50 years.

04

Technology-First

Every improvement starts with one question: how do we give the client more visibility and control? E-Monitoring, milestone dashboards, live CCTV, and material logs — technology in service of trust.

05

The People Behind the Platform

Our 2,000+ partner workforce — engineers, site managers, and skilled tradespeople — build the homes. We invest in their training, safety, and fair payment. Delivery quality is inseparable from how we treat the people delivering it.
