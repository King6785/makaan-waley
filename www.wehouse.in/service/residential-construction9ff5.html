Foundation

Milestones 1–4

  1. 01

    Survey & Soil Test

    Soil & geotechnical report, layout marking, site survey. Foundation depth: 6'6" standard.

  2. 02

    Legal & Permissions

    Plan approval, building permits, layout sanctioning — WeHouse manages all.

  3. 03

    Excavation (JCB/Workforce)

    Pit excavation, PCC layer. Anti-termite treatment (ATT).

  4. 04

    Sub-Structure

    Footings (as per structural drawings), sump RCC 6000L, plinth height 2ft. River sand for brickwork and plaster.

What you receive

  • ✓ Soil & geotechnical report — signed copy
  • ✓ Foundation inspection certificate + depth photos
  • ✓ Material batch delivery log (cement/steel)
  • ✓ Milestone-04 sign-off before structure begins

Structure

Milestones 5–8

  1. 05

    Super-Structure

    RMC M20, columns M20 as per design, beams & slabs. ISI-certified cement. Ceiling height: 10'6" FFL to FFL.

  2. 06

    Brickwork

    Red Brick 9" (external) and 4.5" (internal). River sand mortar. Plumb and level verified per floor.

  3. 07

    Plastering

    River sand plaster. Internal and external — surface quality and thickness inspected before next stage.

  4. 08

    Elevation

    1% of project cost allocated to elevation design. External waterproofing with verified systems. Weatherproof exterior paint.

What you receive

  • ✓ Milestone-08 sign-off before MEP
