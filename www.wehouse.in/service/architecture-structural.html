Our Process

You Sign Off. Then We Proceed.

A stage-gated process where each phase is reviewed and approved before the next begins. No phase runs in parallel without your sign-off.

  1. 01

    Initial Brief

    Plot dimensions, orientation, number of floors, room count and adjacencies, Vastu preferences, and budget range. The brief is the technical document every drawing is produced from — nothing is assumed.

  2. 02

    Concept Design

    2–3 distinct floor plan options with preliminary elevation studies. Each option is a different spatial strategy — not surface variations of the same layout. You choose the direction before any structural work begins.

  3. 03

    Client Review & Revision

    Two rounds of revisions are included. Comments are incorporated within 48 hours. The selected layout is locked before structural engineering starts — protecting both timeline and cost from late-stage changes.

  4. 04

    Structural Engineering

    Column grid, beam sizing, slab design, and reinforcement schedules produced to IS 456 standards. Soil test is coordinated in this phase to confirm foundation type before any structural calculation is finalised.

  5. 05

    Working Drawings

    Full drawing set — architectural, structural, electrical schematic, and plumbing schematic — plus all drawings required for municipal submission. Every sheet is cross-referenced and co-ordinated across disciplines.

  6. 06

    Handover to Construction

    The complete drawing set is transferred to the construction team within the same project management platform. No information is reinterpreted. The drawings that were designed are the drawings that get built from.

Key Differentiator

Design-to-Build. One System. Zero Translation Loss.

Most architecture practices produce drawings for permit approval and hand them to a contractor who wasn't in the room when design decisions were made. What ends up on site is an interpretation of what was drawn — not the drawing itself.

At WeHouse, every architectural and structural deliverable lives inside the same project management platform as construction execution. Drawing revisions, RFIs, and site-level design decisions are resolved within the same system — logged, tracked, and visible. The result is a home that is built to the drawing, not built around it.

Every drawing is produced with construction tolerances in mind. Not just how it looks. How it gets built.
