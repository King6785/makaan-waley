Typical stages include site preparation, foundation, structural drawings and concrete works, waterproofing, electrical and plumbing, and interior finishing. Experienced contractors help each stage stay coordinated so quality and schedule stay aligned.

Most homes take roughly 8–12 months end-to-end, depending on design complexity, number of floors, weather, and team efficiency. Clear planning, milestone tracking, and proactive issue resolution help keep the schedule on track.
