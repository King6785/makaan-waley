Payments — How They Work

MAKAAN WALEY uses milestone-based payments — a clear payment breakup is set against construction milestones so you always know what is due and when. No surprise bills at the end.

  • 012% booking amount — reserves your slot and unlocks detailed design work after consultation.
  • 02Milestone-based payments — payment breakup clearly set as per the 24-milestone framework.
  • 03POS process — every payment is logged in your E-Monitoring dashboard with receipt, stage reference, and running cost tracker.
  • 04Clear schedule — each milestone has a defined payment, with quality sign-off and dashboard updates as work progresses.

Why milestone payments matter

Traditional contractors often demand large ad-hoc advances with little visibility. At MAKAAN WALEY, payments follow a clear milestone breakup — giving you predictability and accountability at every stage.
